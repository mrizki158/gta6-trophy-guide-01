Sources & honesty notes

What is verified, what is not

  • Names, descriptions and grades are the official 55-trophy list as published 2026-11-18 (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze), cross-checked across PowerPyx and GTABase, which report the same list.
  • 48 of 55 guides are verified against a concrete method in at least one cited guide; 2 are partial (Strangers & Freaks — remaining qualifying strands still being confirmed; Fair Game — the exact triggering animal is unconfirmed); 5 are unconfirmed (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) — neither cited guide has the method yet, and those entries say so instead of guessing.
  • Missables: only Positive Mental Attitude is flagged, and only as potentially missable — PowerPyx and GTABase both flag the Chapter 1 balcony meditation, and both say it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy needs it. Every other trophy is marked not missable per the cited guides.
  • Estimates are labelled as estimates: ~200 hours and 4/10 difficulty come from PowerPyx; per-stage hours in the roadmap are estimates built on that figure.
  • Guides are written in our own words from the cited sources. Official trophy names and descriptions are shown as published. No Rockstar artwork, logos or game screenshots are used anywhere in this guide.
