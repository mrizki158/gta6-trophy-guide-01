Sources & honesty notes

What is verified, what is not

  • Names, descriptions and grades are the official 55-trophy list as published 2026-11-18 (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze), cross-checked across PowerPyx, GTABase and Planet GTA 6, which report the same list.
  • 50 of 55 guides are verified against a concrete method in at least one cited guide. Round-3 change: The Truth Is Out There moved from unconfirmed to verified on the evening of 19 November 2026, when PowerPyx's live guide published the full UFO Easter egg method (Kelly County, solo, after 2AM) — its entry now carries the full method.
  • 2 are partial (Strangers & Freaks — Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete are confirmed to count, with their mission lists now in the entry, but more counting strands are still being found; Fair Game — Bret's whole line is mapped, but the exact triggering animal is still unconfirmed).
  • 3 are still unconfirmed (In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, Ten Pin) — re-checked in round 3 against PowerPyx's live guide, GTABase, Push Square's WIP guide and SixShore, and no cited source has a verified method for any of them yet. Those entries collect what each source actually says, with guesses labelled as guesses, instead of inventing steps.
  • Missables: only Positive Mental Attitude is flagged, and only as potentially missable — PowerPyx and GTABase both flag the Chapter 1 balcony meditation, and both say it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy needs it. Every other trophy is marked not missable per the cited guides. The Missable watch view lists the risk, its window and its guard stage.
  • Estimates are labelled as estimates: ~200 hours and 4/10 difficulty come from PowerPyx; the roadmap's seven stages split that same estimate by kind of work, so per-stage hours still add up to 200.
  • Guides are written in our own words from the cited sources. Official trophy names and descriptions are shown as published. No Rockstar artwork, logos or game screenshots are used anywhere in this guide.
